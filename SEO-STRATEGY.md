## Recommendation

Keep the existing audience pages at `/brands` and `/manufacturers`, and the focused service at `/presentation-collections`. Plan two substantial new service pages: `/product-development` and `/manufacturing-sourcing`. Defer `/business-development` until its distinct offer, process and deliverables are established. Do not create `/for-brands` or `/for-manufacturers` alongside their existing equivalents.

This is a relevance and commercial-intent map based on the repository's approved content and your brief. It is not keyword-volume research: search volumes, competition, current rankings and Search Console query data have not been measured. The priority reflects service fit and usefulness to prospective clients, not a prediction of traffic.

## 1. Current architecture and evidence

The site has 14 page types in six languages: 84 public URLs. English is at the root; French, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Spanish and Simplified Chinese have dedicated prefixes. These are real translated pages, not a future multilingual capability that needs rebuilding.

The existing content supports product and collection strategy, creative product direction, new categories, fabric/yarn/material sourcing, factory selection and onboarding, sampling/costing/production, supply chain and manufacturing strategy. Manufacturer services cover development, capability positioning, presentation collections and selective representation. Representation and introductions are conditional, not guaranteed.

About supports fashion/luxury experience and London/Paris relevance. The network section describes manufacturing/material relationships in China, Portugal and Italy and commercial relationships in the UK, France and Brazil. These are not all office locations. Project models are explicitly illustrative, not completed case studies. Field Notes is currently a coming-soon page with no published articles.

The strongest gap is depth on how specific services solve client problems. More combinations of keywords or locations would not solve that gap. Prioritising useful, experience-led content follows [Google's people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
